About the Maison

At Santalum we work with one material: red sandalwood, read by hand and shaped into pieces that keep their record, from a four thousand year tradition to what we make today.

Since 1980 the Maison has worked with red sandalwood and nothing else. Every piece is carved by hand, numbered and registered, and leaves with its provenance documentation.
