The Craft

Select, by grain

Heartwood is chosen block by block, for colour, density and the line of the grain.

01

Shape, by hand

Cuts follow the wood, not a template. The form is set by what the grain allows.

02

Finish, slowly

Surfaces are hand-polished until the colour deepens and the wood feels cool in the palm.

03

Inspect, and number

Each piece is checked, numbered and registered before it leaves the workshop.

04

Present, with its record

Pieces arrive with their material, batch and provenance documentation.
